# Cinder concept development

The packaged atlas is [`../spritesheet.webp`](../spritesheet.webp). The three-pose image under [`../showcase/`](../showcase/) presents the finished pet in Codex and is publication artwork, not an atlas-generation input.

Cinder's approved direction combines faithful blue-heeler proportions and face, restrained mysticism, and a tiny copper code tag. The defining personality feature is the wry side-eye. During final review, apparent character size was normalized across states and the `failed` sequence was repaired so its grounded frames share one common floor reference. Earlier working references and generation intermediates remain outside this public catalogue.

The front-page showcase was composited from three user-supplied Codex screenshots showing alert, failed, and low reviewing poses. Circular interface controls were removed; the dog identity, palette, markings, tag, and expressions were preserved as the presentation target.

After publication, user playback review found Row 7 visually jittery. Timing inspection showed the six active frames were already uniformly paced, while the artwork jumped among low, seated, and upright pose families. The row was therefore regenerated—not merely slowed—as one continuous focused head-and-eye scan. Its final frames share constant height, center, ground, and near-constant width; all non-target atlas rows remain pixel-identical.

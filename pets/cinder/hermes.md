# Cinder

Cinder is a compact blue-heeler familiar for Codex and Hermes Agent: observant, grounded, quietly mystical, and defined by a knowing side-eye.

## Locked identity

- Compact, sturdy blue-heeler anatomy with upright ears and a short, balanced silhouette.
- Black coat with silver-white ticking and ruff, warm tan paws and muzzle points, and a faithful natural face.
- Large brown eyes whose sideways glance carries Cinder's dry, skeptical personality.
- A dark collar with one tiny copper code tag; mysticism stays restrained and never becomes detached spectacle.
- Consistent apparent body size across states and a shared ground reference within every grounded animation.

## Motion language

- `idle`: alert stillness, quiet breathing, ear attention, and the signature side-eye.
- `running-right` / `running-left`: compact literal travel with stable markings and believable foot contact.
- `waving`: a friendly paw greeting that remains canine rather than humanoid.
- `jumping`: a brief, sturdy lift and grounded landing.
- `failed`: disappointment progresses from a slump into a low grounded posture; frames remain on one common floor line.
- `waiting`: patient attention toward the user with restrained ear and eye motion.
- `running`: active agent work expressed through focused bodily attention.
- `review`: a low, scrutinizing pose with the wry side-eye doing most of the storytelling.

## Adaptation guidance

Preserve Cinder's scale, anatomy, markings, copper code tag, and face across every view. Treat the side-eye as the personality anchor. Keep paws grounded unless a state explicitly requires flight, and use one stable character scale for all directional looks. Hermes agents should prefer small readable movements over generic cyber effects.

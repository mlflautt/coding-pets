# Prism

## Identity

Prism is a compact sewn-plush chameleon with emerald looped fleece, oversized physical turret eyes, blue irises in purple rims, orange piping and toes, small orange back spikes, broad yellow-orange-pink-blue saddle bands, and a long purple prehensile tail attached continuously at the rear hip.

## Motion language

- Idle is friendly and alert, using whole-eye rotation, a slight breath, and restrained tail-tip response.
- Directional travel keeps the low plush body and hip-attached tail coherent through an alternating crawl.
- The wave reads through a raised forefoot; jumping moves the complete plush body through a short airborne arc.
- Failure gradually settles into a curled resting pose while preserving the rainbow bands and tail attachment.
- Waiting asks through posture and eyes; active work and review use independent turret-eye attention, head movement, and small plush-body follow-through.
- Looking is led by both complete eye globes, then a restrained head turn or pitch; the feet and tail base remain registered.

## Creative constraints

- Preserve the green fleece, orange piping and toes, rainbow band order, blue-and-purple eye construction, and continuous purple tail.
- The tail must grow visibly from the rear hip, follow a loose curve, and curl only at its slender tip; it may never become detached or shell-like.
- Rotate or redraw complete physical eye globes rather than sliding loose pupils over fixed eyes.
- Do not add labels, props, scenery, shadows, detached effects, or replacement facial features.
- Do not rotate or skew the complete sprite to express gaze.

## Human guidance

The published package is the repaired, user-authorized Prism v2 atlas. It retains the approved plush identity and records the running-left, look-registration, continuity, and chroma corrections that preceded deterministic and independent visual acceptance.

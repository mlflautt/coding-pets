# Little Magus

Little Magus is a soot-black Hermetic bear familiar designed for careful creative and agentic work across Codex and Hermes Agent.

## Locked identity

- Compact upright soot-black plush bear with two arms and two legs.
- Short cream muzzle, amber right eye, and naturally milky-white scarred left eye.
- Tall pale wool Hermetic cap and pale embroidered coat with a rolled braid collar.
- Exactly one connected blackened-silver armillary, controlled by the anatomical-right paw.
- Hand-inked storybook engraving translated into tactile fur, woven wool, and restrained metalwork.

## Motion language

- `idle`: quiet breathing and watchful stillness.
- `running-right` / `running-left`: careful travel while keeping the armillary connected and controlled.
- `waving`: restrained greeting.
- `jumping`: compact lift with stable coat and cap.
- `failed`: sober blocked-work response.
- `waiting`: patient request for approval or guidance.
- `running`: active verification and Hermetic working gestures.
- `review`: armillary-led inspection of completed results.

## Adaptation guidance

Preserve the eye asymmetry, pale cap and coat, sparse three-state embroidery, four-limb anatomy, and single connected armillary. Never duplicate the prop, add detached rings, switch its handedness, or replace the quiet verification ritual with generic wizard effects.

# Little Magus concept development

The packaged atlas is [`../spritesheet.webp`](../spritesheet.webp). The screenshots under [`../showcase/`](../showcase/) show the finished pet in Codex and are presentation evidence, not concept art or atlas-generation inputs.

The approved design locks a soot-black one-eyed Hermetic bear, pale embroidered coat and cap, and one connected armillary held by the anatomical-right paw. Earlier turnaround and expression studies remain in the private hatchery rather than this public catalogue.

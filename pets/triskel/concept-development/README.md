# Triskel concept development

The packaged atlas is [`../spritesheet.webp`](../spritesheet.webp). The screenshots under [`../showcase/`](../showcase/) show the finished pet in Codex and are presentation evidence, not atlas-generation inputs.

Triskel's final design was selected from a user-directed character process and locked around the three brow nodes, wing-shaped ears, gold feather circuitry, four-limb anatomy, and twin cable tails. Earlier working references remain outside this public catalogue; no rejected concepts are presented as shipped artwork.

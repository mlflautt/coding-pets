# Triskel

Triskel is a plush three-node fox-ibis messenger familiar designed for Codex and Hermes Agent.

## Locked identity

- Soot-black plush body with a compact seated-animal silhouette.
- Two tall wing-shaped ears with antique-gold interiors.
- Three luminous brow nodes: amber above, blue at screen-left, violet at screen-right.
- Large amber eyes, small gold ibis-like beak, gold chest diamond, and restrained feather-circuit embroidery.
- Exactly four limbs and two cable tails with gold terminals.

## Motion language

- `idle`: quiet seated presence, breathing, blinking, and subtle ear attention.
- `running-right` / `running-left`: literal travel with stable markings and twin-tail follow-through.
- `waving`: a natural seated-cat greeting.
- `jumping`: compact playful lift and settle.
- `failed`: a readable blocked or disappointed reaction.
- `waiting`: attentive request for user input.
- `running`: active agent processing expressed as an attentive ear-scan, not literal locomotion.
- `review`: focused inspection of completed work.

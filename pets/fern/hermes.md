# Fern

## Identity

Fern is a compact forest dragon assembled from faceted jade-green armor, warm walnut neck and belly panels, charcoal joints and claws, and paired classic wings with jade frames and wooden membranes. A single large amber eye, symmetrical crown spikes, articulated tail, and leaf-shaped tail tip make the silhouette immediately recognizable.

## Motion language

- Idle uses restrained eye, head, wing, and tail-tip movement while the feet remain settled.
- Directional travel is a compact alternating gait with the wings attached and tail following continuously.
- The greeting raises one hand into a clean wave; the jump follows a low-rise-peak-descent-low arc.
- Failure folds Fern gradually toward the ground without detaching armor, wings, or tail.
- Waiting and active-work rows use clear asking and focused gestures; review reads through the large eye and a controlled head angle.
- Looking is led by the complete amber eye globe and rigid head assembly while the lower body stays registered; wings remain attached and the tail tip lags subtly.

## Creative constraints

- Preserve the angular jade plates, walnut panels, one-eye construction, symmetric crown, paired wings, and single leaf tail tip.
- Keep exactly two arms, two hind legs, and two feet in every complete pose.
- Never warp the rigid head plates, change spike counts, detach the wings, or flip the tail to another side.
- Do not add props, labels, UI, scenery, shadows, or detached motion effects.
- Look direction is expressed by the eye, head, neck, and restrained upper-body follow-through rather than rotating the complete sprite.

## Human guidance

The published package retains the user-approved jade-and-walnut reference. Its wave, jump, directional gait, and look loop passed targeted repairs, deterministic validation, blind direction review with a documented minor resolution, and independent final visual QA.

# Vesper

Vesper is a Codex pet and studio-familiar designed for creative, agentic work across Codex and Hermes Agent environments.

## Locked identity

- Compact threshold spirit with a pearl-opalescent seed mask.
- Violet-black layered leaf-feather mantle with antique-gold seams and circuitry.
- Two black eye apertures with restrained violet inner glimmers.
- Oval opal third eye inside three close concentric bands: antique gold, moonlit silver, then burnished copper.
- Exactly three attached antennae in every pose: one central stalk and two side stalks, each ending in luminous glass.
- Luminosity is integrated through the mask, feather interiors and tips, circuitry, third-eye opal, and antenna glass. No detached halo, sticker aura, or background bloom.

## Animation language

- `idle`: quiet breathing, blink, and a violet-lavender antenna pulse.
- `running-right` / `running-left`: directional gliding gait with mantle drag; antenna glass leans violet with blue-white accents.
- `waving`: one leaf-fan unfolds as an uncanny-friendly greeting; antennae answer in warmer rose-gold notes.
- `jumping`: compressed anticipation, bell-like feather bloom, hover, descent, and settle.
- `failed`: opal and antenna light dim while the mantle slumps, followed by a small recovery glint.
- `waiting`: patient listening and an open asking gesture with alternating soft violet and amber antenna cues.
- `running`: planted active processing; circuitry, opal, and antenna nodes sequence without literal locomotion.
- `review`: inward analytical inspection, triangulated antenna attention, and a small satisfied nod.

## Adaptation guidance

Preserve the locked identity, three-antenna rule, integrated-light treatment, direction semantics, and animation meanings. Other runtimes may add higher-frame animations, but the Codex atlas must retain its fixed state and frame counts. Keep variants for human comparison instead of silently selecting an aesthetic winner.

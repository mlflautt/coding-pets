# Vesper concept development

This folder records selected visual exploration behind Vesper. It is intentionally separate from the shipped atlas and real-use preview.

## Authority

- Actual packaged pet: [`../spritesheet.webp`](../spritesheet.webp)
- Actual-use screenshot: [`../preview.png`](../preview.png)
- Character identity and motion language: [`../hermes.md`](../hermes.md)

Concept images may be more detailed, dramatic, or aspirational than the installed animation. They must never be presented as proof of in-app appearance.

## Iteration log

No concept images are published here yet. The previously generated four-panel showcase experiments remain excluded at the user's request because they made Vesper appear cooler and more elaborate than the actual pet animations.

When adding an iteration, record:

| ID | File | Role | Origin | Outcome | Relationship to final pet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `concept-001` | `concept-001-short-name.png` | exploration | human / generated / hybrid | candidate / approved-reference / rejected | Concise factual note |

Keep IDs stable. Do not silently delete rejected directions that remain useful to understanding the creative process.

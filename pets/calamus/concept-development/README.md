# Calamus concept development

The packaged atlas is [`../spritesheet.webp`](../spritesheet.webp). The image under [`../showcase/`](../showcase/) presents the finished pet in Codex and is publication artwork, not an atlas-generation input.

Calamus began as Concept 3, Faience Calamus: a natural mantis-like familiar built from warm pale faience, copper-gold plate seams, and carnelian eyes. The approved direction channels Thoth through writing, memory, measurement, and verification without literal deity costume or detached symbolic props.

[`canonical-turnaround-v2.png`](canonical-turnaround-v2.png) is the approved identity reference. It superseded a narrower first turnaround by restoring substantial cranium, thorax, abdomen, hip, and wing-case volume in profile. [`expression-mechanics.png`](expression-mechanics.png) is the approved behavior reference for eyes, antennae, writing forelimbs, and agent states.

Strict animation review later found profiles where a far walking leg disappeared behind a near leg. Both directional eight-frame gait families were regenerated as whole rows. The published result keeps all four walking legs separately countable alongside the two folded writing forelimbs in every directional frame.

The three-pose showcase was composed from the retained Codex screenshots `pose-01.png`, `pose-02.png`, and `pose-03.png`. It is a repository presentation asset; the validated spritesheet remains authoritative.

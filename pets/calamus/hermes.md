# Calamus

Calamus is a warm ivory-celadon faience mantis familiar for Codex and Hermes Agent. He embodies the spirit of Thoth through disciplined attention, transcription, comparison, measured reasoning, and verified execution without depicting the deity or wearing Egyptian costume.

## Locked identity

- Compact upright mantis with a broad triangular head, large translucent carnelian-orange eyes, rounded abdomen, and two closed attached wing cases.
- Warm pale faience shell with sparse copper-gold plate seams and restrained geometric wing veins.
- Exactly six limbs: four separately countable walking legs plus two folded blunt writing forelimbs.
- Exactly two attached segmented antennae. No tail, clothing, handheld prop, headdress, detached pen, floating glyph, or extra wing.
- Profile views retain substantial cranium, thorax, abdomen, hip, and wing-case depth; never collapse Calamus into a narrow blade silhouette.

## Motion language

- `idle`: serene attention, a small blink, subtle thorax breath, and gentle antenna follow-through.
- `running-right` / `running-left`: deliberate directional travel with all four walking legs visibly countable in every frame; the writing forelimbs remain folded and attached.
- `waving`: one friendly anatomical forelimb gesture with no motion marks.
- `jumping`: frontal crouch, rise, and matching crouch return.
- `failed`: dignified disappointment through lowered head, softened antennae, and protective forelimb fold.
- `waiting`: a paired, gentle question pose toward the user.
- `running`: active agent work expressed as measured transcription rather than literal locomotion.
- `review`: eyes and head inspect laterally while the loop returns cleanly to front.

## Adaptation guidance

Limb-count continuity is a hard gate: every new view and animation frame must show four walking legs plus two writing forelimbs, with far legs staggered enough to remain readable in profile. Preserve the pale faience, copper seams, orange eyes, two antennae, paired wing cases, broad silhouette, and calm intelligent demeanor. Express agentic behavior through attached anatomy and surface-bound material changes, never detached effects or readable symbols.

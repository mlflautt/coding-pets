# Moon Regent

Moon Regent is a calm Emperor penguin familiar for Codex and Hermes Agent. He joins Thoth-Hermes lunar and scribal symbolism with a natural penguin silhouette, translating agent work into measured movement, contained insight, attentive review, and verified recovery.

## Locked identity

- Tall, columnar Emperor penguin with one continuous natural avian body, exactly two flippers, two legs, two webbed feet, and one attached tail wedge.
- Black head and back, warm cream-gold breast and throat patches, amber eyes, and a short species-correct penguin beak. Never restore ibis anatomy or lengthen the beak.
- One papyrus hood and two cream papyrus drapery panels ending halfway down the body, edged in aged gold.
- One turquoise-and-gold collar with one attached lapis throat seal.
- One moonstone held physically inside an aged-gold crescent crown. The stone may brighten internally but never grows, floats, emits a halo, or produces detached rays.
- No humanoid hands, extra limbs, loose pages, detached glyphs, scenery, shadows, or new props.

## Motion language

- `idle`: quiet breathing, one slow blink, and restrained papyrus sway while the crown remains steady.
- `running-right` / `running-left`: a compact royal courier waddle with alternating webbed feet, flipper counter-swing, and drapery lagging the body by one phase. Revision 3 derives the leftward gait frame-by-frame from the accepted rightward cadence without reversing time.
- `waving`: one flipper rises through a formal Hermetic salutation and returns through the same controlled arc.
- `jumping`: compact crouch, rise, airborne peak, controlled descent, and cushioned landing without body scaling.
- `failed`: the crown stone dims, the normal-proportioned head lowers in reflection, then the penguin inhales and recovers.
- `waiting`: an attentive head tilt and one open flipper ask for user guidance.
- `running`: active work appears as contained spiritual insight—the crown moonstone brightens internally, followed by the attached throat seal, then both settle.
- `review`: eyes and head inspect screen-right and screen-left in equal, restrained measures while skull, beak, and crown proportions remain fixed.

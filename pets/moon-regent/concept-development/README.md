# Moon Regent concept development

The packaged atlas is [`../spritesheet.webp`](../spritesheet.webp). The public [`../preview.png`](../preview.png) is reproduced from three shipped atlas cells by [`../preview.json`](../preview.json), so it depicts the installed pet rather than aspirational concept art.

[`four-penguin-approved-reference.png`](four-penguin-approved-reference.png) records the user-directed species exploration that translated the original hooded Moon-Scribe ibis into four distinct penguin types. Reading left to right, the concepts were Moon Regent (Emperor), Relay Fledgling (Little Blue), Glyph Runner (Southern Rockhopper), and Archive Diver (African). The user selected the first concept. The other three remain historical alternatives and were not blended into Moon Regent.

[`canonical-base.png`](canonical-base.png) is the approved identity reference for the selected Emperor penguin: tall natural anatomy, amber eye, short penguin beak, moonstone-and-crescent crown, turquoise-gold collar, lapis seal, and papyrus hood with half-length drapery.

Animation revision 2 replaced generic actions and repaired waving, jumping, failed, and review continuity while preserving the approved 16-direction loop. After actual playback exposed a stilted leftward gait, revision 3 changed only `running-left`. Its eight frames were derived independently from the accepted rightward stride with frame order preserved, producing a readable alternating foot cadence and a clean loop seam. The user approved this result and authorized catalogue publication.
